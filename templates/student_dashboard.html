Privacy Policy

How CampusFix handles your information.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information we collect

To run the complaint system we collect your name, university roll number, class, year and Gmail address, along with the details you submit in a complaint: category, title, description, location and any photo you attach.

2. How we use it

  • To register you and verify your Gmail through OTP
  • To route your complaint to the right warden or department
  • To categorise and prioritise complaints using automated models
  • To notify you about status changes until your complaint is resolved

3. Who can see your complaints

Only you, the assigned warden and the university administration can view your complaints and photos. Your details are never sold or shared with third parties outside the university.

4. Photos and location

Photos are used only as evidence for the issue you report. Location is captured only when you tap the location button and is used just to fill the address field. Please avoid photographing people without their consent.

5. Data security

Passwords are stored in hashed form and OTPs expire shortly after being issued. Access to complaint records is limited by role — student, warden and admin.

6. Data retention

Complaint records are kept for the duration of your enrolment so that repeat issues can be tracked, and are removed or anonymised afterwards.

7. Your choices

You can edit your profile, delete a complaint that has not yet been reviewed, or log out at any time. For anything else, contact the university administration or your hostel warden.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, reach out to the CampusFix team at Shobhit University through your department office.

University guidelines

  • Report ragging or harassment to the Anti-Ragging Committee / Squad, the Proctor or the Dean of Shobhit University.
  • For sexual harassment, approach the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) of the university.
  • Hostel students can also inform the warden immediately.

UGC guidelines

  • UGC Regulations on Curbing the Menace of Ragging in Higher Educational Institutions, 2009.
  • UGC Regulations on Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal of Sexual Harassment of Women Employees and Students in HEIs, 2015.
  • You can also file a complaint with the UGC Anti-Ragging Helpline (1800-180-5522) or online.
